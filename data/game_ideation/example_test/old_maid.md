## Old Maid

### Setup

* Use a standard 52-card deck.
* Remove **one Queen** from the deck so that one Queen remains unmatched.
* Shuffle the cards and deal **all cards evenly** to the players (2+ players).

---

### Objective

Form matching pairs and avoid being left with the single unmatched Queen — the **Old Maid**. 

---

### Gameplay

1. **Discard starting pairs**

   * Each player looks at their hand.
   * Any matching pairs (same rank) are placed face up on the table.
   * If a player has three of a kind, they discard only one pair and keep the extra card.

2. **Taking turns**

   * Starting with the player to the left of the dealer:

     * They offer their hand face down to the next player.
     * The next player selects **one random card** from it.

3. **Form new pairs**

   * If the drawn card matches one already in their hand:

     * The pair is placed face up.
   * If not:

     * The player keeps the card.

4. **Continue play**

   * The player who just drew a card now offers their hand to the next player.
   * Turns continue in this way around the table. 

---

### Ending the Game

Play continues until all possible pairs are laid down.

One card will remain unmatched — the Queen without a pair.

* The player holding this card is the **Old Maid**.
* That player **loses the game**.
