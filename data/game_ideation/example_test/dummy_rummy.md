## Dummy Rummy

### Setup

* **2–4 players** 
* Use **two standard decks plus 4 Jokers** (108 cards total). 
* Twos and Jokers are **wild cards**. 
* Deal **13 cards to each player**. 
* Place the remaining cards face down as the **stock** and start a **discard pile**. 

---

### Objective

Complete the required meld for each hand and be the player with the **lowest total penalty score after 12 hands**. 

A round ends when a player gets rid of all their cards. 

---

### Melds

You form:

* **Sets** – cards of the same rank
* **Runs** – consecutive cards (same suit not required) 

Wild cards may substitute for any card. 

---

### Required Melds by Hand

Each round has a specific goal:

1. Two 3-of-a-kinds
2. 3-of-a-kind + run of 4
3. Two 4-of-a-kinds
4. Two runs of 4
5. 4-of-a-kind + run of 4
6. Two 3-of-a-kinds + run of 4
7. 3-of-a-kind + run of 7
8. Three 3-of-a-kinds
9. Two 5-of-a-kinds
10. Two runs of 5
11. Eight-of-a-kind
12. Run of 10 

---

### Gameplay

Players take turns clockwise.

On your turn:

1. **Draw**

   * Take the top card from the stock, or
   * Take the top discard. 

2. **Meld**

   * Lay down the required meld if possible.
   * After completing it, you may add cards to existing melds. 

3. **Discard**

   * End your turn by discarding one card. 

---

### Ending the Round

The round ends when a player:

* Melds all required combinations, and
* Discards their final card. 

---

### Scoring

All other players score the value of cards left in hand:

* 3–9 = 5 points
* 10, J, Q, K = 10 points
* Ace = 15 points
* 2 or Joker = 50 points 

Scores are added after each round.

---

### Winning the Game

After completing all 12 hands:

👉 The player with the **lowest total score wins**.
