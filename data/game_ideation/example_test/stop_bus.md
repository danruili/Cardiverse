## Stop the Bus

### Setup

* **3+ players**
* Use a standard **52-card deck**. 
* Each player starts with **3 tokens** (lives). 
* Deal:

  * **3 cards face down to each player**
  * **3 cards face up** in the center of the table. 

---

### Objective

Finish the round with the **best 3-card Brag hand**.

The player with the weakest hand loses a token. 

---

### Gameplay

1. **Player Turns**

   Starting with the player to the dealer’s left and moving clockwise:

   * On your turn, you may swap:

     * **1 card** or
     * **all 3 cards**

     with the face-up center cards. 

2. **Stopping the Bus**

   * Instead of swapping, you may say:

     **“Stop the Bus.”**

   * After this, all other players get **one final turn**. 

3. **End of Round**

   * After the final turns, all players reveal their hands. 

---

### Hand Rankings (High → Low)

1. **Three of a kind (Prial)**
2. **Running Flush** (same suit, consecutive)
3. **Run** (consecutive)
4. **Flush** (same suit)
5. **Pair**
6. **High Card** 

---

### Losing the Round

* The player with the **lowest-ranking hand** loses one token. 

---

### Winning the Game

* Continue playing rounds.
* Players who lose all tokens are eliminated.
* The **last player with a token remaining** wins.
