## Yaniv

### Setup

* **2–5 players**
* Use a **54-card deck** (standard deck + 2 jokers). 
* Deal:

  * **5 cards to each player**
  * Place the remaining cards face down as a **draw pile**
  * Turn the top card face up to start the **discard pile** 

---

### Objective

Have the **lowest total hand value** when a round ends. 

---

### Card Values

* Joker = 0
* Ace = 1
* 2–10 = face value
* Jack, Queen, King = 10

---

### Gameplay

Play proceeds clockwise.

On your turn, you must:

1. **Discard first**, then
2. **Draw one card**

You may discard:

* A single card
* A set of cards of the same rank
* A sequence of 3+ consecutive cards of the same suit

After discarding, draw:

* The top card of the draw pile, or
* The first or last card from the previous discard set/sequence

---

### Calling “Yaniv”

At the start of your turn, if your hand totals **5 points or less**, you may call:

**“Yaniv!”**

* The round ends immediately.
* All players reveal their hands.

---

### Scoring

* If the caller has the **lowest total** → they score **0 points**
* All other players score the value of their hands. 

If another player has an equal or lower total than the caller:

* The caller receives a **30-point penalty** plus their hand value. 

---

### Winning the Game

* Scores are added after each round.
* Any player reaching **over 200 points** is eliminated.
* The **last remaining player** wins.
