## German Rummy

### Setup

* **2+ players**
* Use a standard **52-card deck with Jokers**. 
* Deal **13 cards to each player**. 
* Place the remaining cards face down as the **stock**.
* Turn the top card face up to begin the **discard pile**. 

---

### Objective

Be the first player to **meld all your cards** by forming valid sets or runs. 

---

### Melds

You may form:

* **Set** – 3 or 4 cards of the same rank
* **Run** – 3 or more consecutive cards in the same suit 

Jokers are wild and may substitute for any card. 

Aces may be:

* Low (A-2-3), or
* High (Q-K-A) 

---

### Gameplay

Play proceeds clockwise.

On your turn:

1. **Draw**

   Take either:

   * The top card from the discard pile, or
   * The top card from the stock 

2. **Meld**

   You may lay down valid sets or runs.

   Your first meld must total **at least 40 points**. 

3. **Lay Off**

   After your first meld, you may add cards to:

   * Your own melds, or
   * Other players’ melds 

4. **Discard**

   End your turn by discarding one card face up. 

---

### Joker Rule

If a meld contains a Joker, a player holding the real card it represents may:

* Replace the Joker, and
* Immediately use the Joker in a new meld. 

---

### Ending the Round

The round ends when a player:

* Melds or lays off all cards, and
* Discards their final card while declaring **“Rommé.”** 

All other players score penalty points based on the cards remaining in their hands. 

---

### Winning the Game

* Continue playing rounds.
* The player who finishes a round first wins that round.
* The overall winner is the player with the lowest total penalty points.
