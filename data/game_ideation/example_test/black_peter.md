## Black Peter

### Setup

* 2 or more players.
* Use a special Black Peter deck consisting of:

  * Matching pairs of cards
  * One extra card — **Black Peter** (which has no pair).
* Shuffle and deal **all cards evenly** to the players.

---

### Objective

Discard all your cards by forming matching pairs and avoid being left with the **Black Peter** card.

---

### Gameplay

1. **Discard starting pairs**

   * Each player checks their hand.
   * Any matching pairs are placed face up and removed from play.

2. **Drawing cards**

   * The player to the left of the dealer begins.
   * They draw one card from the player to their left.

3. **Make new pairs**

   * If the drawn card forms a pair with a card in hand:

     * The pair is discarded.

4. **Continue play**

   * The next player repeats the same action.
   * Players keep drawing cards and discarding pairs. 

---

### Ending the Game

Eventually, all pairs are removed.

* One player will be left holding the single unmatched card — **Black Peter**.
* That player loses the game.
