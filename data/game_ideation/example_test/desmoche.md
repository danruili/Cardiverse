## Desmoche

### Setup

* **2–4 players**
* Use a standard **52-card deck**. 
* Players place an agreed stake into a pot before the deal. 
* Deal **9 cards to each player**.
* Place the remaining cards face down as the **deck**. 

---

### Objective

Be the first player to lay down **exactly 10 cards** in valid melds (runs or sets). 

---

### Melds

You may play:

* **Run** – 3+ consecutive cards of the same suit
* **Set** – 3 or 4 cards of the same rank 

Runs must be in ascending order.

Valid examples:

* A-2-3
* 5-6-7
* Three Kings 

---

### Gameplay

1. **Exchange (Cambio)**

   Before play begins:

   * Each player passes **one card face down** to the player on their right. 

2. **Turns**

   Starting with the player to the dealer’s right and moving counter-clockwise:

   On your turn:

   * Draw one card from the deck. 

   If you can use it:

   * Immediately play it as part of a meld on the table. 

   If not:

   * Discard it face up. 

   Other players may take a discarded card if they can use it to complete a meld. 

3. **Hand Size Rule**

   You must always have **9 total cards** between:

   * Cards in hand
   * Cards played in front of you 

   If you draw or take a card, you must discard to return to nine. 

4. **Desmoche Move**

   You may take a card from one of your own melds and reuse it in another —
   as long as all remaining melds stay valid. 

---

### Ending the Round

The round ends when a player:

👉 Plays **10 cards in melds**.

That player wins the hand. 

If the deck runs out before anyone does this:

* There is **no winner**, and the stake carries over to the next round. 

---

### Winning the Game

Play multiple hands.

The overall winner is the player who wins the pot before leaving the game.
