## Golf (Six-Card)

### Setup

* **2+ players**
* Use a standard **52-card deck**. 
* Deal **6 cards face down** to each player. 
* Each player arranges their cards in **two rows of three**.
* Turn **any two cards face up**.
* Place the remaining cards face down as a **stockpile**.
* Turn the top card of the stock face up to start the **discard pile**. 

---

### Objective

Have the **lowest total score** at the end of the round. 

---

### Gameplay

Starting with the player to the dealer’s left and moving clockwise:

1. **Draw**

   On your turn, draw one card from either:

   * The stockpile, or
   * The discard pile. 

2. **Swap or Discard**

   After drawing, you may:

   * Swap the drawn card with one of your six cards, or
   * Discard it.

   If you swap with a face-down card:

   * The new card stays face up. 

   If you discard:

   * You may flip one of your face-down cards face up. 

3. **Discard Rule**

   * A card taken from the discard pile must be used — it cannot be discarded immediately. 

---

### Ending the Round

* The round ends when a player has **all six cards face up**.
* Other players may receive one final turn.
* All cards are then revealed and scored. 

---

### Scoring

* Ace = 1 point
* 2 = –2 points
* 3–10 = face value
* Jack = 10 points
* Queen = 10 points
* King = 0 points 

Special rule:

* A **pair of equal cards in the same column = 0 points** for that column. 

---

### Winning the Game

* Play multiple rounds (called “holes”).
* After the agreed number of rounds, the player with the **lowest total score** wins.
