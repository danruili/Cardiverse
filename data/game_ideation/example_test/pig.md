## Pig

### Setup

* 3 or more players.
* Use a standard deck of cards.
* Deal **4 cards to each player**.

---

### Objective

Be the first player to collect **four cards of the same rank** (e.g. four Kings, four 7s). 

---

### Gameplay

1. **Passing cards**

   * Each player simultaneously selects **one card** from their hand.
   * All players pass that card face down to the player on their left.

2. **Continue passing**

   * Players pick up the card they receive and repeat the process.
   * This continues quickly and continuously.

3. **Getting four of a kind**

   * When a player collects **four cards of the same rank**, they:

     * Quietly place a finger on their nose.

4. **Reaction**

   * When other players notice someone touching their nose, they must also do the same.

---

### Losing the Round

* The **last player** to notice and place their finger on their nose becomes the **Pig**. 

---

### Winning the Game

* Play several rounds.
* The player who becomes the Pig the **fewest times** is the winner.
