## Kemps

### Setup

* **4 players** in **2 teams of 2**
* Teammates sit opposite each other.
* Use a standard **52-card deck**. 
* Each team secretly agrees on **one non-verbal signal** that means:

  👉 “I have four of a kind.” 

---

### Objective

Be the first team to successfully:

* Get **four cards of the same rank**, and
* Have your partner call **“KEMPS!”** 

The losing team receives a penalty letter.
The first team to spell **K-E-M-P-S** loses the game. 

---

### Deal

1. The dealer gives:

   * **4 cards to each player**
   * **4 cards face up** in the center of the table 

---

### Gameplay

1. **Free-for-all trading**

   * Players may take **one or more** face-up cards.
   * They must immediately discard the same number.
   * Players always keep **4 cards in hand**.
   * No turns — play happens simultaneously. 

2. **Refresh the center**

   * When no one wants the face-up cards:

     * The dealer replaces them with **4 new face-up cards**. 

3. **Signal**

   * When a player gets **four of a kind**, they secretly signal their partner. 

4. **Call KEMPS**

   * If the partner notices, they shout:

     **“KEMPS!”**

   * The signaling player reveals their hand.

   * If correct → opposing team receives a letter. 

5. **Counterplay**

   * If an opponent suspects a team has four of a kind, they may shout:

     **“STOP KEMPS!”**

   * If correct → that team receives a letter.

   * If wrong → the calling team receives a letter. 

---

### Winning the Game

* After each round, the losing team receives a letter.
* The first team to spell:

  **K-E-M-P-S**

  loses the game.
