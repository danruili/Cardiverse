## James Bond

### Setup

* **2–4 players**

* Use a standard **52-card deck** (no jokers). 

* Deal the cards into **face-down piles of 4**:

  * 2 players → 6 piles each
  * 3 players → 4 piles each
  * 4 players → 3 piles each 

* Place the **remaining 4 cards face up** in the center. 

---

### Objective

Be the first player to turn **all your piles into four-of-a-kind** (four cards of the same rank).

---

### Gameplay

1. **Simultaneous Play**

   * There are **no turns**.
   * All players play at the same time.

2. **Working on a Pile**

   * Pick up one pile of four cards and look at it.
   * You may swap **one card at a time** with a face-up card in the center.

   Rule:

   * You must discard to the center **before** taking a new card.
   * There must always be:

     * 4 cards in your pile
     * 4 cards in the center 

3. **Switching Piles**

   * You may put a pile down at any time.
   * Then pick up another pile to work on.
   * You may only look at **one pile at a time**.

4. **Completing a Pile**

   * When a pile becomes four-of-a-kind, place it face down and move to another pile.

---

### Winning the Game

* When **all your piles** are four-of-a-kind:

  * Turn them face up and shout:

  **“James Bond!”** 

* The first player to do this wins.
