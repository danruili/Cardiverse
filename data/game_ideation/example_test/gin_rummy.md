## Gin Rummy

### Setup

* **2 players**
* Use a standard **52-card deck**. 
* Deal **10 cards to each player**.
* Place the remaining cards face down as the **stock**.
* Turn the top card face up to start the **discard pile**. 

---

### Objective

Form combinations (**melds**) and reduce unmatched cards (**deadwood**).

Win the game by reaching **100 points first**. 

---

### Melds

You may form:

* **Set** – 3 or 4 cards of the same rank
* **Run** – 3+ consecutive cards of the same suit 

A card may belong to only one meld.

Aces are low (A-2-3 is valid; Q-K-A is not). 

---

### Card Values

* Ace = 1
* Face cards = 10
* Others = face value 

---

### Gameplay

Players alternate turns.

On your turn:

1. **Draw**

   Take the top card from either:

   * The stock, or
   * The discard pile 

2. **Discard**

   Discard one card.

   You must always keep **10 cards** in hand. 

---

### Ending the Round

A round ends when a player:

#### Knock

You may knock when your deadwood totals **10 points or less**.

* Reveal your hand.
* Opponent may add matching cards to your melds.
* Compare deadwood totals. 

If the knocker has less deadwood → they score the difference.

If the opponent has equal or less → opponent scores the difference plus bonus (undercut). 

---

#### Go Gin

If all 10 cards form melds (no deadwood):

* Reveal immediately.
* Opponent cannot add cards.
* Score opponent’s deadwood + bonus. 

---

### Drawn Round

If only two cards remain in the stock without a knock:

* The round ends with **no score**. 

---

### Winning the Game

* Continue playing rounds.
* The first player to reach **100 points** wins.
