## Coucou

### Setup

* 5–20 players
* Use a 32-card or 52-card deck
* Each player receives **one card only**
* Each player also receives the same number of chips (for example, five) 

Card ranking (high → low):

**King → Queen → Jack → 10 → … → 2 → Ace**
(The Ace is always the lowest card.)

---

### Objective

Avoid being the player with the **lowest card** at the end of the round. 

---

### Gameplay

1. **Dealer advantage**

   * The dealer is dealt **three cards**.
   * The dealer keeps the highest card and returns the other two to the deck.
   * All other players receive one card.

2. **Player actions**
   Starting with the player to the dealer’s right and moving around:

   Each player may either:

   * **Keep** their card
     OR
   * **Exchange** it with the player to their right.

3. **King rule**

   * A player holding a King may refuse an exchange.
   * They announce **“Coucou!”** and no exchange happens. 

4. **Last player**

   * The last player exchanges with the top card of the deck instead of a neighbour.
   * If this card is a King, no exchange is allowed. 

5. **Dealer**

   * The dealer keeps the card chosen earlier and does not exchange.

---

### End of the Round

* All players reveal their cards.
* The player with the **lowest card** loses the round and places a chip into the center. 

Players who lose all their chips are eliminated.

---

### Winning the Game

Play continues until only **one player remains with chips**.

That player wins the game.
