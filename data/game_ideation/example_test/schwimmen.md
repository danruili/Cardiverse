## Schwimmen

### Setup

* **2–9 players**
* Use a **32-card deck** (7–Ace). 
* Each player starts with **3 lives** (tokens). 
* Deal:

  * **3 cards to each player**
  * **3 cards face up** in the center (the pool). 

---

### Objective

Finish the round with the **highest-value hand**, ideally totaling **31 points in one suit**. 

The player with the lowest score loses a life.

---

### Card Values

* Ace = 11
* King, Queen, Jack, 10 = 10
* 9, 8, 7 = face value

Only cards of **one suit** count toward your score.

Example:

* A♣ + K♣ + 9♣ = 30 points

Special hand:

* **Three of a kind = 30 points** 

---

### Gameplay

Starting with the player to the dealer’s left and moving clockwise:

On your turn, you may:

1. **Swap one card** with a card from the pool
2. **Swap all three cards** with the pool
3. **Pass** and keep your hand

---

### Knocking (Ending the Round)

Instead of swapping, you may **knock** if satisfied with your hand.

* All other players get **one final turn**. 
* Afterward, all hands are revealed.

---

### Losing a Life

* The player with the **lowest score** loses one life. 
---

### Winning the Game

* Players eliminated after losing all lives. 
* The **last remaining player** wins.
