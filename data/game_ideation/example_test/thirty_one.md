## Thirty-One

### Setup

* **2–8 players**
* Use a standard **52-card deck**. 
* Each player starts with **3 lives** (often coins or tokens). 
* Deal:

  * **3 cards to each player**
  * Place the remaining cards face down as a **stock**
  * Turn the top card face up to start the **discard pile** 

---

### Objective

Finish the round with cards of **one suit totaling as close to 31 as possible**.

The player with the lowest score loses a life. 

---

### Card Values

* Ace = 11
* King, Queen, Jack = 10
* All other cards = face value 

Only cards of **one suit** count toward your score. 

Example:

* A♥ + K♥ + 10♥ = 31

---

### Gameplay

Starting with the player to the dealer’s left and moving clockwise:

1. **Draw**

   On your turn, take the top card from either:

   * The stock, or
   * The discard pile 

2. **Discard**

   After drawing, discard one card face up.

   You must always end your turn with **3 cards**. 

3. **Knock**

   Instead of drawing, you may **knock** if you believe your hand is strong.

   * All other players get **one final turn**. 

4. **Blitz**

   If you ever reach **exactly 31 in one suit**, reveal immediately.

   * The round ends instantly. 

---

### End of Round

All players reveal their hands.

* The player with the **lowest score** loses one life.
* If the knocker has the lowest score, they lose **two lives**. 

---

### Winning the Game

* Players who lose all lives are eliminated.
* The **last remaining player** wins.
