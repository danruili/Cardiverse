## One-Card Kille (Swap Kille)

### Setup

* **3–12 players**
* Use a traditional **42-card Kille deck**
* Before each deal, every player places an equal stake into a pot.
* The dealer gives **one card face down to each player**.  

---

### Objective

Avoid having the **lowest-ranking card** at the end of the round.

The player with the lowest card is eliminated.  

---

### Gameplay

1. **Turn Order**

   * Play proceeds clockwise starting with the player to the dealer’s left.  

2. **On Your Turn**

   You must choose one of the following:

   * **Switch** – Exchange your card with the player to your left.
   * **Satisfied** – Keep your card and end your action.  

3. **Special Card Reactions**

   If you attempt to switch with a player holding one of these cards:

   * **Cuckoo** – The round ends immediately and all cards are revealed.
   * **Hussar** – The player attempting the switch is eliminated.
   * **Pig** – The exchange is cancelled, previous exchanges involving that card are undone, and a player is eliminated.
   * **Cavalier / Inn** – You must skip that player and attempt to switch with the next one.  

4. **Dealer’s Turn**

   * The dealer may switch only with the remaining deck (stock).  

5. **End of the Round**

   The round ends when:

   * All players have taken a turn, or
   * A **Cuckoo** is revealed.  

---

### Elimination

After all cards are revealed:

* The player with the **lowest card** is eliminated.
* Players already knocked out by special cards are also eliminated.  

---

### Winning the Game

* Continue playing new rounds.
* The **last remaining player** wins the game.
