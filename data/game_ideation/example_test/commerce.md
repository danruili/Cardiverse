## Commerce

### Setup

* **3–10 players**
* Use a standard **52-card deck**. 
* Each player contributes equally to a pot.
* Deal:

  * **3 cards to each player**
  * **3 cards face up** in the center — this is the **widow**. 

---

### Objective

Finish the round with the **best three-card hand**. 

---

### Gameplay

1. **Dealer’s option**

   Before looking at their hand, the dealer may swap **1–3 cards** with the widow. 

2. **Player turns**

   Starting with the player to the dealer’s left and continuing clockwise:

   * On your turn, you may swap **1–3 cards** with the widow.
   * If you swap all three cards at once, you cannot swap again later. 

3. **Rounds**

   * The game proceeds in rounds.
   * At the start of each round, **one new card is added** to the widow. 

4. **Knocking**

   * If satisfied with your hand, knock instead of swapping.
   * When **two players have knocked**, play stops. 

---

### Hand Rankings (High → Low)

1. **Tricon** – Three of a kind
2. **Sequence** – Three cards in suit and sequence
3. **Flush** – Three cards of the same suit
4. **Pair** – Two cards of the same rank
5. **Point** – Highest total value

   * A = 11
   * Face cards = 10
   * Others = face value 

---

### End of Round

* All players reveal their hands.
* The player with the **best hand wins the pot**. 

---

### Winning the Game

* Continue playing rounds.
* The player who remains in the game the longest (after others lose their counters) wins.
