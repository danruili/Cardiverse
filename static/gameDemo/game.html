Go Fish Instructions

Ask any opponent for any rank. If they have it, they give you all cards of that rank and you keep playing. If not, you draw from stock.

Goal

Collect the most books (four cards of the same rank).

Turn Overview

  • Choose a target player and a rank to request.
  • If successful, receive all matching cards and continue your turn.
  • If unsuccessful, Go Fish: draw one card, then turn passes.
  • Any set of 4 matching ranks becomes a revealed book immediately.

Misdirection Rule

  • You may request ranks you do not currently hold.
  • You may use requests to mislead opponents and infer their hands.

Game End

The game ends when all 13 books are collected, or when stock is exhausted. Most books wins.
